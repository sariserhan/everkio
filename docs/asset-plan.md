## Acquisition and rights record required

Before purchasing/importing, record the legal licensee, named art/engineering owner, all-in budget approval, exact asset/version and shader dependencies, receipt, dated license terms and publisher provenance. Preserve raw source and a SHA-256 inventory privately; commit only rights records safe to share and import instructions. Do not place licensed source in a public repository.

The [Unity EULA FAQ](https://assetstore.unity.com/browse/eula-faq) describes commercial use as embedded in a larger product and excludes separately downloadable/extractable asset distribution. The [actual EULA](https://unity.com/legal/as-terms), exact package terms and intended delivery must be reviewed before acquisition. Current listings show Standard Unity Asset Store EULA, but inconsistent Single Entity/Extension labels require checkout clarification. Embedded app use, future downloadable animal bundles and any export of editable animal assets must each have documented rights. Do not assume generic asset rights permit reconstruction/training or independent raw-asset exports. No vendor has been contacted and no legal clearance is claimed.
